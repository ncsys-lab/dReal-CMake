# alglib (module)
ALGLIB-backed **non-rigorous** eigenvalue/eigenvector routines for general matrices (`computeEigenvalues`, `computeEigenvaluesAndEigenvectors`, complex-to-string helpers). Not interval/rigorous; unused by dReal (`dreal-capd-usage.md`). Source: [group__alglib.html](../../../CAPD/docs/html/group__alglib.html)
