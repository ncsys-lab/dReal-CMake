# mpcapdlib (module)
Multiprecision (`MpFloat`-endpoint) instantiations of CAPD sets, solvers, and QR reorganization policies (`MpC0Set`, `C0Rect2Policies`, …). dReal uses `CAPD_INTERVAL_TYPE=NATIVE` (double), not multiprecision — unused (`dreal-capd-usage.md`).
